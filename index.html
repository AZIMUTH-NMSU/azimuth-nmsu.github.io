From solar activity to satellite behavior, AZIMUTH Lab combines observations, physical modeling, and AI across three connected areas of expertise.

AZIMUTH expertise

01 / AZIMUTH

Space weather

Solar observations, flare precursors, and AI-enabled nowcasting. Solar-cycle and geomagnetic forecasting, including 20–30 day storm-warning research. Applications to GNSS, radio communications, and infrastructure resilience.

02 / AZIMUTH

Space domain awareness

Satellite orbit prediction and LEO/VLEO behavior during space-weather events. TLE population analysis, ballistic coefficients, atmospheric drag, and orbital decay—connecting environmental conditions to mission planning.

03 / AZIMUTH

Atmospheric science

Thermospheric density and storm-time atmospheric response using Swarm observations and satellite data. Atmospheric gravity waves, eclipse and balloon studies, and coupling across the Sun–Earth system.

AI, education & workforce development · SOLAR-AI research, interdisciplinary student training, short courses in space weather and space domain awareness, and NASA SciAct heliophysics learning for Grades 4–8.
